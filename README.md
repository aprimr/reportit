# ReportIt

ReportIt is a community complaint management system developed as part of a fourth-semester academic project. It aims to simplify the process of reporting community issues, managing complaints, and tracking their progress through a digital platform.

The system allows community members to view complaints submitted by others, report local issues, and monitor complaint progress. Administrators can review, verify, reject, and manage complaints through a centralized interface.

## Features

### User Module
- View complaints submitted by other community members.
- Submit complaints with descriptions, images and location.
- View complaint details and current status.
- Track complaints and monitor their progress.
- View complaint information to improve community awareness.

### Admin Module
- View and manage submitted complaints.
- Review complaint details and supporting images.
- Verify or Reject submitted complaints.
- Update complaint statuses and provide relevant remarks.
- Manage complaints through a centralized interface.

## Tech Stack

- ***Mobile Application***: **Flutter**, **Riverpod**, **Hive**, **Google Maps**
- ***Backend***: **Go**, **PostgreSQL**, **Cloudinary**

## Reporting Workflow

1. A community member submits a complaint through the application.
2. The system creates a complaint record in the database.
3. An administrator reviews the submitted information and supporting images.
4. The administrator verifies the complaint or rejects it with an appropriate reason.
5. Verified complaints proceed through the resolution process.
6. The complaint status is updated as the issue progresses.
7. Community members can use the available tracking functionality to check complaint details and status.
